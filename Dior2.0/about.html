THE PROJECT / THE POINT OF VIEW

An ode to the details.

A student-led digital fashion experience reimagining the luxury editorial for a new generation.

01 / THE IDEA

Not just a website.
An experience.

DIOR 2.0 began as an exploration of how a familiar fashion house's visual language could be reinterpreted through the web. Rather than treating fashion as a static catalogue, this concept brings together storytelling, motion, tactile interaction and careful typography.

At its centre sits an interactive lookbook inspired by the feeling of leafing through a printed fashion magazine.

02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Designed to feel different.

01

Editorial first

Photography, storytelling and pacing set the rhythm, rather than an endless product grid.

02

Interaction with purpose

Page turning, intuitive filters, saved favourites and responsive navigation make discovery feel natural.

03

Accessible by design

Keyboard-friendly controls, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support keep the experience welcoming.

AN IMPORTANT NOTE

Independent by design.
